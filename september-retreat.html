A full-body warm oil massage customized to your constitution, traditionally used to support relaxation and circulation.

Warm medicated oil poured in a steady stream over the forehead for 20–30 minutes, traditionally used for deep relaxation and calming the nervous system.

Understanding Your Program

The Philosophy Behind the Plan

What Is Ayurveda

The classical medical system that originated in India, in continuous use for more than 5,000 years — using herbal medicine, massage therapy, diet guidance, and cleansing therapy (Panchakarma) to treat and prevent illness, aiming to cure without side effects. Its hallmark is personalization: two guests with the same apparent imbalance may still need different treatment to correct it — which is why every program here starts with a diagnosis, not a checklist.

What Is Panchakarma — Five Actions, Three Stages

Not a spa treatment but a supervised medical process, done in three stages:

01

Preparation (Poorvakarma)

Getting the body ready for the cleanse is essential. Internal oleation, oil and sweating therapies, and herbal preparations loosen deep-seated toxins.

02

Elimination (Pradhanakarma)

The 5 cleanses are Vamana (therapeutic emesis), Virechana (therapeutic purgation), Basti (enema), Nasya (nasal cleanse), and Raktamokshana (bloodletting) — only medically prescribed according to your health circumstances.

03

Rejuvenation (Paschatkarma)

Last, but not least, the recovery. Herbs, diet, and rest rebuild strength and stabilize the results.

Why Asturias — Spain's Natural Paradise

Not Mallorca, Not Kerala, Not Germany

Cooler air, green hills, Spanish town culture, a quiet river estuary — not September heat and beach crowds. The same Kerala tradition, without the long-haul flight to India.

More Reasons to Choose the North Coast

Climate & Nature

Asturias is officially branded "Paraíso Natural" (Natural Paradise) — green hills, the Picos de Europa mountains nearby, and a noticeably cooler, milder September than the Mediterranean coast. Better conditions for rest and recovery than a hot beach resort.

Heritage & Culture

Home to Oviedo's UNESCO-listed pre-Romanesque architecture, the historic Camino de Santiago pilgrim route running through the region, and the charming fishing village of Cudillero close by.

Slower, Less Touristy

Asturias sees a fraction of the mass tourism of Mallorca or the Costa Brava — a quieter, more authentic setting matched to what a real recovery program needs.

Where You'll Stay — San Esteban de Pravia

A quiet former coal port turned into a peaceful river village on the Nalón estuary — local gastronomy, fishing boats, seafront promenades, and a walk to wild beaches and impressive coastal cliffs. Close by to the town of Muros de Nalón, part of the Camino de Santiago.

Approximate time by car: Asturias Airport ~10 min · Avilés ~18 min · Oviedo and Gijón ~35 min
