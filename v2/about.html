World Health Organization

Setting Global Standards for Ayurveda

As interest in Ayurveda grows outside India, the need for safe, standardized, and authentic training is crucial. Vaidya Vijay is a proud member of the World Health Organization (WHO) expert committee on Ayurveda training.

He helped develop the international guidance on how Ayurveda should be taught and practiced outside India. The same rigorous standards that guide global doctors will guide your personal consultation and courses.

About Kerala Ayurveda Institute of Barcelona (KAIB)

Founded by Vaidya Vijay, KAIB is more than a clinic. It is a center for genuine Ayurvedic education and care. While our roots began in Barcelona, our services now extend across Spain—with online courses for global students, and a dedicated clinic in Madrid for in-person treatments and pulse reading.

Ayurveda works hand in hand with your modern doctor: your doctor cares for your acute conditions, while we care for your everyday balance and lifestyle.
