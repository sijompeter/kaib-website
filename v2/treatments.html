FAQ

Before you book

Do I need a consultation first?

For your first visit we recommend it, so the treatment fits your body type. Some therapies, like Pizhichil, Nasyam or Netra Tharpanam, are only given as part of a plan. To confirm with Vaidya Vijay

Can I combine treatments?

Yes. Thalam, for example, is used with almost all oil massages. Vaidya Vijay can suggest the right combination. To confirm

Is there anything I should tell you beforehand?

Please let us know if you are pregnant, have a fever or a recent injury, or take regular medication. Ayurveda complements, and does not replace, care from your doctor. To confirm
